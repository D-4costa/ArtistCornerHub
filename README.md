# Artist Corner Hub

## Overview

Artist Corner Hub is a platform designed to help digital artists showcase their work, build their portfolios, and connect with potential clients. The project aims to create a space where artists can share their creativity, gain visibility, and explore opportunities to grow professionally.

The platform focuses on making it easier for artists to present their work in an organized and accessible way while helping potential clients discover artists whose styles and skills match their needs.

## Project Purpose

The purpose of Artist Corner Hub is to provide digital artists with a dedicated platform to showcase their artwork and connect with people interested in their services.

Many artists use different platforms to share their work, but having a space focused on portfolios and artistic services can make it easier to organize their projects and reach potential clients.

## Main Features

The planned features of Artist Corner Hub include:

- **Artist Profiles:** Allow artists to create profiles and introduce themselves, their skills, and their artistic interests.
- **Digital Portfolios:** Provide a space for artists to showcase their artwork and previous projects.
- **Artwork Categories:** Organize artwork into categories to make browsing easier.
- **Artist Discovery:** Help users explore different artists and their creative styles.
- **Client Connections:** Support connections between artists and potential clients interested in their work.

These features may evolve as the project develops and requirements are defined.

## Target Audience

Artist Corner Hub is designed for:

- Digital artists who want to showcase their work.
- Artists who are building their professional portfolios.
- Freelancers looking for potential clients.
- People interested in discovering and commissioning digital artwork.

## Project Goals

The main goals of this project are to:

- Create an organized and accessible platform for digital artists.
- Help artists present their work professionally.
- Improve the visibility of independent artists.
- Make it easier for potential clients to discover artistic talent.
- Develop a functional and user-friendly application.
